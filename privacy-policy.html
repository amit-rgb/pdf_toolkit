Privacy Policy for PDF Toolkit

Effective date: October 10, 2026

PDF Toolkit (the “App”) is published by Abhisadan Private Limited (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This policy explains how information may be handled when you use the App.

1. Files and information you choose to use

The App provides PDF-related tools. Files you select or create may contain personal or confidential information. Use the App only with files you are authorized to access, and review the permissions requested by your device.

PDF processing features are intended to operate on your device unless a feature clearly indicates that information is being sent to an online service. Your selected files may be read, modified, or created to provide the feature you request. We do not claim that every feature is entirely offline.

2. Online and AI-powered features

The App includes software dependencies that can support Firebase services, including Firebase AI and App Check. The exact data processed depends on which online or AI-powered features are enabled and used in the version you install. When you use such a feature, text, prompts, or other information you submit may be transmitted to Google/Firebase or another service provider to process your request. Those providers may process technical information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information or confidential documents to an AI or online feature unless you are comfortable with that information being processed by the relevant service. We must update this policy if the App's enabled services or data practices change.

3. Information collected automatically

The App's integrated services may process technical information needed to provide, protect, and troubleshoot a feature, such as app/device details, diagnostic information, service request metadata, or abuse-prevention signals. We have not verified that every such category is collected in every release. We do not state that the App collects no data.

4. How information is used

Information processed by the App or its service providers may be used to provide requested PDF or AI functionality, maintain service security, prevent abuse, and diagnose technical problems. We do not sell your personal information.

5. Sharing

Information may be processed by service providers only as needed for features you use, security, or technical operations. This policy does not mean that every feature sends files off your device; whether information is transmitted depends on the feature and its implementation.

6. Storage, retention, and deletion

Files you create or save may remain in the storage location you choose on your device until you delete them. Information processed by third-party services is subject to the relevant provider's retention practices. We cannot promise deletion from a third-party service without confirming that provider's controls and the App's implementation.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps appropriate to the App to protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Keep your device protected and avoid sharing sensitive files with features whose processing behavior you do not understand.

8. Children

The App is not designed specifically for children under 13, and we do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through an online feature, contact us through the repository link below.

9. Your choices

You can choose which files to open and which optional online features to use. You can delete files you have saved using your device's file-management tools. You may also choose not to use AI or online features if you do not want information submitted to those services.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App, its services, or applicable requirements change. The effective date at the top indicates the latest version of this page.

11. Contact

Publisher: Abhisadan Private Limited

For privacy questions or requests, contact the publisher through the PDF Toolkit GitHub repository. Please do not post personal or sensitive information publicly in an issue.
